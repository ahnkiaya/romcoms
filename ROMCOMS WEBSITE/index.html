Notting Hill ♡

A charming bookstore owner falls in love with a famous Hollywood actress. A sweet romantic comedy about love, fame, and unexpected connections.

27 Dresses ♡

Jane Nichols is a kind-hearted woman who has been a bridesmaid 27 times but has never been the bride herself. When her younger sister becomes engaged to the man Jane secretly loves, she must confront her feelings while dealing with a charming journalist who wants to write a story about her endless bridesmaid experiences.

Genre: Romance • Comedy

How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days ♡

Andie Anderson, a magazine writer, takes on an assignment to make a man fall out of love with her in just 10 days. Meanwhile, Benjamin Barry, an advertising executive, bets that he can make any woman fall in love with him in the same amount of time. When they meet, both have their own agendas, leading to hilarious misunderstandings, romantic moments, and unexpected feelings.

Genre: Romance • Comedy

A Cinderella Story ♡

Sam Montgomery, a hardworking high school student living under the control of her cruel stepmother, dreams of escaping her difficult life. When she meets her online crush at a school dance, she must discover his identity while dealing with unexpected romance, high school drama, and the challenges of finding her happily ever after.

Genre: Romance • Comedy

10 Things I Hate About You ♡

Cameron, a new student, falls for Bianca, but her strict father only allows her to date if her rebellious older sister, Kat, dates first. Cameron arranges for the mysterious Patrick to win Kat's heart, leading to unexpected romance, hilarious moments, and lessons about love and honesty.

Genre: Romance • Comedy

Flipped ♡

Juli Baker has been in love with her neighbor, Bryce Loski, since they first met as children. While Juli sees Bryce as someone special, he finds her annoying and tries to avoid her. As they grow older, their feelings begin to change, teaching them about love, friendship, and seeing people for who they truly are.

Genre: Romance • Comedy • Coming-of-Age • Teen

She's the Man ♡

When Viola's girls' soccer team is cut, she disguises herself as her twin brother and enrolls at his boarding school to prove herself on the boys' soccer team. But things get complicated when she falls for her roommate, leading to hilarious misunderstandings, unexpected romance, and a journey of self-discovery.

Genre: Romance • Comedy • Sports

The Princess Diaries ♡

Mia Thermopolis, an ordinary and shy teenager, discovers that she is the heir to the throne of a small European kingdom. With the help of her grandmother, she undergoes royal training while navigating high school drama, friendship, and unexpected romance as she learns what it means to be a princess.

Genre: Romance • Comedy • Teen Comedy • Family

Mamma Mia ♡

Sophie, a young woman preparing for her wedding on a beautiful Greek island, secretly invites three men from her mother's past, hoping to discover which one is her father. As old romances resurface and secrets unfold, the family finds themselves caught in a joyful adventure filled with love, laughter, and unforgettable ABBA songs.

Genre: Romance • Comedy • Musical
